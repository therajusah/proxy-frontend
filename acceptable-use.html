acceptable use

Shared infrastructure needs shared rules.

RelayNorth is for authorized browsing and testing. The development build is intentionally allowlisted.

Never use the service for

  • Unauthorized access, scanning, probing, or vulnerability testing.
  • Spam, phishing, malware, credential theft, or denial-of-service attacks.
  • Illegal content, harassment, threats, or privacy violations.
  • Copyright infringement or attempts to defeat a destination’s technical protections.
  • Excessive automated crawling, hotlinking, or resource consumption.

Response

We may block destinations, suspend sessions, preserve limited abuse evidence, and cooperate with lawful requests. Report suspected abuse through the deployment’s published contact address.
