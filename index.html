Why use a relay?

Simple access, clear limits.

A relay is an intermediary. It can change which server an approved destination sees, but it cannot erase identity from a logged-in account or protect every app on your device.

01 / privacy

Protect your tab

Pages are fetched by the relay instead of directly by your browser, so the approved destination sees the relay’s address.

02 / speed

Keep the page intact

Links, styles, forms, and assets are rewritten back through the session whenever the destination supports it.

03 / regions

Choose a viewpoint

Pick a relay region and test how an approved destination responds from that network.

04 / limits

Know what changes

This is a tab-level relay, not a device VPN, and it does not promise complete anonymity.
