privacy note

Less data is a feature.

This development slice keeps operational state in memory and does not persist browsing bodies.

What we process

Active sessions contain an opaque identifier, selected relay cell, approved origin, expiry, request count, and temporary cookie state. Usage counters are aggregate operational metrics.

What we do not want

Do not send passwords, payment details, health information, or other confidential data through a development relay. The proxy is not a secure vault.

Retention

Development sessions expire after 15 minutes. Production retention, legal bases, data-subject rights, vendors, and international transfers must be finalized before public launch.
