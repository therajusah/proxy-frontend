copyright contact

Rights concerns belong in the open.

This placeholder explains the intended process before a production legal review.

Report

Send a written notice to the deployment’s designated copyright contact with your signature, identification of the protected work, the location of the material, contact details, and the required good-faith and accuracy statements.

Counter-notice

Where applicable, a counter-notice process and repeat-infringer policy must be published and operated by the legal entity running the service.

Important

A proxy operator should not assume that a generic disclaimer removes copyright obligations. Obtain counsel for the jurisdictions and service model involved.
